Sponsor Shield is ready

Open any YouTube video. Sponsor segments marked by the SponsorBlock community are muted and blurred, and you can always choose Show anyway.

Optional

AI checks

Most videos aren’t in SponsorBlock yet. With AI checks on, when SponsorBlock has nothing for a video, Sponsor Shield reads that video’s captions and has an AI find the sponsor parts.

What gets sent

  • To the Sponsor Shield server, run by the developer: the video ID, its captions, its public description, whether YouTube labels it as paid promotion, and a random ID this extension makes.
  • The server passes the captions and description to DeepSeek, an AI provider, to find the sponsors.

What doesn’t

  • Your Google account, your watch history, or anything from other websites.
  • The server keeps the sponsor times it found. It doesn’t keep the captions or your IP address.

You can change this any time with the AI checks switch in the extension’s popup. Full details are in the privacy policy.
